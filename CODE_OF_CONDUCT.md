# WALI Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in WALI respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, ability, or role. This applies to human contributors and to people directing autonomous agents.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-oriented.
- Critique designs and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while still checking technical claims.
- Credit prior work and preserve contributor history.
- Accept correction and repair harm promptly.
- Keep automated contributions attributable to a responsible human account.
- Respect privacy, licenses, security boundaries, and user safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Plagiarism, license violations, impersonation, or hiding automated authorship.
- Flooding maintainers with generated changes that were not reviewed or verified.
- Using project spaces to distribute malware, stolen media, credentials, or bypass instructions.
- Retaliation against someone reporting a concern in good faith.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may edit, reject, remove, or restrict contributions and participation when needed to protect the community or project. Enforcement should be proportional, documented privately, and free of conflicts of interest.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. A monitored private
conduct contact has not yet been published in this policy; request a private
route without posting incident details or personal information. Do not use a
public issue to disclose a sensitive report. Maintainers must publish and verify
a route before public community launch, as recorded in the
[publication checklist](docs/maintainers/publication.md). Reports should be
handled as confidentially as practical; this policy promises no response deadline.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone publicly represents WALI, including issue trackers, reviews, community chats, events, and release channels.
