# WALI Copyright Policy

**Version:** 2026-09-01
**Status:** Draft for counsel review; production agent/contact not configured

WALI respects copyright and requires creators to hold redistribution rights.
A notice should identify the copyrighted work, the exact WALI content or URL,
the reporting party's contact information, a good-faith statement, an accuracy
and authority statement, and a physical or electronic signature.

WALI may temporarily block downloads or delist content while reviewing a
complete notice. The uploader may submit a counter-notice containing the
required identification, contact, consent-to-jurisdiction, good-faith mistake
statement, and signature. Restoration occurs only when legally appropriate.

WALI records notices, actions, deadlines, and repeat-infringer decisions with
restricted access. Public issues must not be used for notices containing
personal information. A designated agent, governing jurisdiction, mailing
address, and private production intake address must be approved by counsel and
published before public creator uploads are enabled.
