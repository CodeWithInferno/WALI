The marketplace records the chosen license, rights holder, source,
attribution, terms revision, and approved release. WALI does not silently
broaden that license.

A creator may request delisting. Cached or previously downloaded copies may
remain on user devices where the applicable license permits. WALI may retain
bounded evidence and audit records for legal and security purposes. WALI may
immediately block a release or signing key when safety or rights are in doubt.

The exact termination, indemnity, jurisdiction, age, and entity provisions
require counsel approval before creator uploads are enabled.
