# WALI Content Guidelines

**Version:** 2026-09-01
**Status:** Draft for counsel review

WALI accepts wallpaper media only when it can be safely processed and lawfully
redistributed. Approval is discretionary and can be reversed.

Public creator uploads are currently disabled. The local service accepts only
`original` and `public_domain` declarations without proof objects; licensed or
other submissions remain unavailable until the proof workflow is implemented,
reviewed, and enabled. The requirements below are the public-beta policy target.

## Required

- Accurate title, description, category, source, rights holder, license, and
  attribution.
- Rights evidence appropriate to the selected license.
- A license that permits marketplace redistribution and end-user wallpaper use.
- Media within the published duration, codec, dimension, and file-size limits.
- An appropriate content rating and truthful disclosure of generated media.

## Not allowed

- Malware, exploit payloads, scripts, executables, polyglots, malformed media,
  tracking pixels, or attempts to escape the processing sandbox.
- Content copied from another wallpaper service, private endpoint, paywall,
  DRM system, or account without explicit redistribution permission.
- Copyright or trademark infringement, non-consensual intimate imagery,
  sexual content involving minors, doxxing, private data, threats, targeted
  harassment, extremist recruitment, fraud, or illegal content.
- Misleading metadata, engagement manipulation, repeated evasive submissions,
  or resubmission of removed material without authorization.

Moderators review canonical artifacts, not raw uploads in the wallpaper player.
Decisions are revision-bound and audited. Users and creators may appeal through
the production support channel once it is configured.
