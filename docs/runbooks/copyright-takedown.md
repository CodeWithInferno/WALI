# Copyright takedown

**Owner:** rights operator
**Approver:** designated legal reviewer

1. Accept notices only through the private configured channel and validate the
   required work, content, contact, authority, good-faith, accuracy, and
   signature fields.
2. Create a restricted case ID. Store personal notice data outside public
   catalog/audit payloads.
3. Preserve the exact release, rights declaration, source, attribution, and
   moderator history. Delist/block downloads when the policy or law requires;
   do not rewrite the immutable release.
4. Notify the creator through the recorded channel and provide the applicable
   counter-notice process without sharing unnecessary reporter data.
5. Apply restoration, continued removal, or account action only after the
   required waiting period and legal review. Record the policy/document version
   and decision actor.
6. Critical revocation is reserved for security or urgent legal risk; ordinary
   delisting must not remotely delete already downloaded local files.

Track repeat-infringer decisions consistently and accessibly. Exit when all
deadlines, notifications, catalog state, retained evidence, and appeal status
are recorded.
